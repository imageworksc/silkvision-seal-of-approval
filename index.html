Office-Based Surgery

A New, Advanced Option for Convenient and Affordable Eye Care

Your doctor has determined that you are an excellent candidate for office-based surgery (OBS). This means that your procedure can be performed in our state-of-the-art surgical suite right here in our office, utilizing local anesthetics—no need for a hospital or ambulatory surgery center (ASC).

Experience the Silk Vision Difference with OBS

At Silk Vision & Surgical Center, we take pride in delivering a superior surgical experience tailored to your needs. By performing procedures in-office, we provide a more personalized, comfortable, and convenient option than traditional hospital settings or multi-specialty surgery centers.

What Sets OBS Apart?

  • Safety: Our suite meets the highest safety standards, minimizing exposure to unfamiliar environments.
  • Comfort: Receive care from the team you know and trust, in a familiar setting.
  • Streamlined Care: Enjoy greater scheduling flexibility and reduced stress with shorter recovery times.
  • One Location Convenience: On-site surgery reduces the need for travel.

Why Patients Choose Office-Based Surgery

Patients who opt for OBS enjoy these outstanding benefits:

  • No Fasting: Eat before your surgery—no need for extended fasting.
  • No IV Required: Say goodbye to the discomfort of an IV.
  • Convenient Location: All services are provided in one location—our office.
  • More Scheduling Options: Get the procedure done on a timeline that works for you.
  • Faster Recovery: Less time in the recovery area means you’re on your way home sooner.
  • Less Stress: A simpler process, designed to make you feel at ease.

Advanced Surgical Options Available at Silk Vision

We offer a range of advanced eye surgeries, with a focus on delivering exceptional results for your vision:

Cataract Surgery

Regain clear vision with this life-changing procedure that replaces your clouded natural lens with an artificial intraocular lens (IOL).

Surgery Day: What to Expect

Your entire visit will take approximately 90 minutes. Here’s how to prepare and what you can expect:

  1. Have a friend or family member drive you to our office.
  2. Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
  3. Check-in: Our staff will review the details with you.
  4. Preparation: Get prepped for surgery by our caring team.
  5. Procedure: Your surgery will take only a few minutes.
  6. Recovery: Rest in our recovery area before heading home.
  7. Aftercare Instructions: Receive detailed guidance on caring for your eye.
  8. Go home: Once you feel ready, your friend or family member can drive you home.
