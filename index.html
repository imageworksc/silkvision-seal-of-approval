Surgery day

What to expect, step by step

Your entire visit will take approximately 90 minutes. Here’s how to prepare and what you can expect.

  1. Your ride

    Have a friend or family member drive you to our office.

  2. What to wear

    Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

  3. Check in

    Our staff will review the details with you.

  4. Preparation

    Get prepped for surgery by our caring team.

  5. The procedure

    Your surgery will take only a few minutes.

  6. Recovery

    Rest in our recovery area before heading home.

  7. Aftercare instructions

    Receive detailed guidance on caring for your eye.

  8. Go home

    Once you feel ready, your friend or family member can drive you home.
